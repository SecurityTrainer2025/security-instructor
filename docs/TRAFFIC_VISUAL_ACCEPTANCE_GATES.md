## Exact job identity
The protagonist is a PRIVATE SECURITY COMPANY employee assigned/qualified as a Traffic Marshal, not a police officer and not a generic road worker. Within the scope of site procedures: navy security trousers and shirt, appropriate navy cap or mandated hard hat, high-visibility yellow reflective vest, visible radio and safety shoes. ## Frame-level gates (every narrated segment)
1. **Subject** — Does the shot show an identifiable private security Traffic Marshal with required PPE? If not, mark as background context only (maximum 2 seconds) or REJECT.
2. **Action** — Is the protagonist visibly performing the exact verb in the narration, e.g., briefing driver, checking TMP, clearing crossing, coordinating gate by radio? Generic traffic, empty road or workers posing DO NOT pass.
3. **Vehicle/pedestrian response** — Where the voice mentions a movement instruction, show a visible coordinated and safe response, and a marshal standing at a safe position. If footage lacks this response, REJECT as procedural demonstration.
4. **Truthfulness** — Do not digitally re-label police or construction personnel as security officers without authentic, clearly disclosed reenactment, and do not imply a stock actor endorses SI.
5. **Safety** — No reversing/hand-signal demonstration unless an appropriately qualified instructor verifies the sequence against the target-site approved method. Until then label explicitly “theory-only / شرح نظري” and withhold procedural approval.
6. **Sound** — Keep approved Arabic voice where applicable; only re-time subtitles after final edit to exact speech, not guessed timing.
7. **Approval** — A passed technical codec/audio test is NOT visual/educational approval; leave all `SI_TRAFFIC_FINAL_MEDIA` flags `approved:false` until per-screen owner sign-off.
