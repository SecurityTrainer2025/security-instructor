# SI Traffic — Lesson 1 voiceover production script (staging only)

**Status:** Editorial script approved for pilot preparation; audio **not yet recorded or published**. The current 60-second MP4 is silent. No paid voice service is required to use this script.

**Lesson:** Introduction to Site Traffic Control / مقدمة في التحكم بحركة المرور بالمواقع الحيوية

**Production direction:** Natural, professional Arabic narration, medium pace; English subtitles. Five scenes, 12 seconds each. Avoid absolute instructions that override site risk assessments, applicable law, or authorized traffic control procedures.

| Time | Scene | Arabic voiceover | English subtitle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:12 | Why traffic management matters | أهلاً بكم في دورة إدارة حركة المرور. هدفنا الأول حماية الأشخاص وتنظيم حركة المركبات بصورة آمنة وواضحة. | Welcome to Traffic Management. Our first priority is protecting people and keeping vehicle movement safe and predictable. |
| 00:12–00:24 | Gates and access | عند البوابات، تحقق من التصاريح وفق إجراءات الموقع المعتمدة، وحافظ على مسارات الدخول والخروج واضحة. | At gates, check access authorization under approved site procedures and keep entry and exit lanes clear. |
| 00:24–00:36 | Pedestrians | انتبه دائمًا لعبور المشاة. افصل مساراتهم عن المركبات، واتبع ترتيبات العبور الآمن المعتمدة بالموقع. | Watch for pedestrians. Keep their routes separate from vehicles and follow approved crossing arrangements. |
| 00:36–00:48 | Parking and emergencies | نظّم حركة المواقف، ولا تسمح بإغلاق مسارات الطوارئ أو إعاقة وصول مركبات الاستجابة. | Manage parking movements and keep emergency access routes unobstructed. |
| 00:48–01:00 | Officer safety | احرص على وضوح رؤيتك للسائقين، وتجنب النقاط العمياء، ولا تعمل خارج خطة المرور وتقييم المخاطر المعتمدين. | Stay visible to drivers, avoid blind spots, and work within the approved traffic plan and risk assessment. |

## Audio acceptance checklist

- Arabic is pronounced clearly, without misreading security or traffic terms.
- Narration is paced so each scene finishes within its 12-second window.
- English captions match the intended meaning; check mobile legibility.
- Voiceover is intelligible on phone speakers and does not clip.
- Audio/video synchronization and browser playback are tested on staging.
- Retain the silent pilot until a narrated version passes QA; never imply audio exists before then.
- Production deployment and legacy courses are out of scope.
