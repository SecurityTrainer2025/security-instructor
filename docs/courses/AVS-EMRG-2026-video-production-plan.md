# AVS-EMRG-2026 — Video Production Plan

**Course:** Safety, Emergency & Evacuation in Civil Airports / إدارة السلامة والطوارئ والإخلاء في المطارات المدنية  
**Code:** AVS-EMRG-2026  
**Status:** Production plan only. No video is marked published until a final file and approved hosting URL are supplied and tested.

## Production standard
- Eight short videos, generally 90–180 seconds each; concise, scenario-led, not long lectures.
- 16:9, 1080p MP4 (H.264), clear narration, readable on-screen labels, captions/transcripts in Arabic and English.
- Visual identity: navy #0B1F33, dark navy #081725, secondary navy #102A43, gold #C8A96B.
- Use original motion graphics or licensed footage only. Do not use real restricted-area maps, security-sensitive airport layouts, unverified incident footage, or unlicensed third-party clips.
- All incidents are labelled **SIMULATION / سيناريو افتراضي**. Follow the airport emergency plan and competent authority direction in real events.

## Video list and storyboards

### AVS-V01 — A civil airport as a connected system (90 sec)
**Placement:** Screen 1.  
**Purpose:** Show how terminal, apron, service roads, utilities, passengers, staff, and emergency responders depend on one another.  
**Sequence:** 0–15s airport overview; 15–45s animated system links; 45–70s example of a disruption affecting adjacent functions; 70–90s key message: protect life, communicate clearly, follow the plan.  
**Arabic narration focus:** المطار منظومة مترابطة؛ قد يؤثر حادث في منطقة واحدة على مناطق أخرى، لذلك تعد سلامة الأرواح والتواصل واتباع الخطة أولويات أساسية.

### AVS-V02 — Who does what during an airport emergency? (150 sec)
**Placement:** Screen 3.  
**Purpose:** Explain command, airport operations, security, fire and rescue, medical support, and communications without assigning universal authority to one role.  
**Sequence:** alert received; information verified; designated command structure activated; tasks communicated; updates and handover recorded.  
**Safety note:** Roles and command authority vary by airport plan and jurisdiction.

### AVS-V03 — From hazard to controls: a simple Bowtie (180 sec)
**Placement:** Screen 5.  
**Purpose:** Explain hazard, threats, top event, consequences, preventive controls, and recovery/mitigation controls.  
**Sequence:** fictional ground-equipment overheating example; identify threats; show barriers; discuss what happens if a barrier fails; learner pauses to suggest an additional control.  
**On-screen prompt:** “Which control prevents the event, and which reduces its consequences?”

### AVS-V04 — A clear emergency radio message (120 sec)
**Placement:** Screen 7.  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate calm, concise reporting.  
**Sequence:** poor vague message; improved message containing location, observed hazard, immediate risk, action already taken, and help needed; repeat-back/confirmation.  
**Safety note:** Use site-approved radio phraseology and channels; do not invent real airport frequencies or codes.

### AVS-V05 — Choosing a safe movement strategy (180 sec)
**Placement:** Screen 8.  
**Purpose:** Compare evacuation, partial evacuation, and shelter-in-place as decisions made under the approved plan by authorized incident leadership.  
**Sequence:** fictional decision point; show accessible routes and crowd-flow principles; identify people who may need assistance; assembly/headcount; controlled re-entry only after all-clear.  
**Safety note:** No one-size-fits-all instruction; conditions and command decisions govern.

### AVS-V06 — Simulated APU/ground-equipment fire (150 sec)
**Placement:** Screen 9.  
**Purpose:** Teach recognition, immediate reporting, keeping clear, and following trained responder direction.  
**Sequence:** visible warning sign; label “SIMULATION”; report; protect route access; responders take control; post-event area release.  
**Safety note:** Do not teach unqualified learners to approach an aircraft fire, enter smoke, or operate specialist equipment.

### AVS-V07 — Suspicious-object scenario (120 sec)
**Placement:** Screen 11.  
**Purpose:** Teach non-contact, withdrawal according to instructions, protecting people, and reporting via official channels.  
**Sequence:** item noticed; do not touch or investigate; notify through approved process; follow directions; keep public movement orderly.  
**Safety note:** Do not depict exploitable device details, search methods, or sensitive response positions.

### AVS-V08 — Tabletop exercise briefing (90 sec)
**Placement:** Screen 13.  
**Purpose:** Brief learners on a fictional combined scenario and their decision task.  
**Sequence:** establish fictional locations; present two simultaneous reports; ask learners to prioritize life safety, reporting, route protection, verified information, and authorized decisions; pause for learner response.  
**Debrief:** discuss communication, role clarity, safe access, accountability, and improvement actions.
