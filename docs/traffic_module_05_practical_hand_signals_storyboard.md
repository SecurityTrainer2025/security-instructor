# SI Traffic — Module 05: Practical Hand Signals & Driver Communication
# الوحدة الخامسة: التطبيق العملي لإشارات اليد والتواصل مع السائقين

**Status:** Production storyboard and safety acceptance criteria for isolated pilot. This is NOT an approved universal hand-signal standard.

## Essential teaching requirement
The lesson video must show an **animated human demonstrator** from front and side, with full upper body and both hands visible. Static titles, a still diagram, or voiceover-only footage **do not meet** the practical demonstration requirement. Show the driver's view and vehicle response in a controlled training scene. Narration: approved clear, natural Modern Standard Arabic; synchronized English captions must translate the complete narration. All lesson explanations, decisions, feedback and assessment are Arabic and English.

## Safety boundary
Hand signals vary by jurisdiction, road authority and site procedure. Obtain and verify the approved local/site signal chart before publishing specific movement gestures. Do not invent a universal 'slow down', 'come forward', 'turn left/right', or 'reverse' gesture. A stop signal is not a substitute for physical traffic controls or authorized traffic direction. Never direct live public-road traffic without legal authority. No one stands in a vehicle's path or blind spot.

## 5-minute practical video storyboard (15 x 20-second segments)
1. Introduction: safe positioning, visibility and approved signal chart.
2. Demonstrator: neutral ready posture, safe escape route; front and side view.
3. Demonstrator: **STOP**—show only the verified site-approved signal, freeze and replay.
4. Driver perspective: STOP recognition and complete stop at designated point.
5. Demonstrator: **PROCEED**—show only verified site-approved signal, freeze and replay.
6. Driver perspective: controlled forward movement and safe clearance.
7. Demonstrator: **SLOW**—show only verified site-approved signal, freeze and replay.
8. Driver perspective: speed reduction; never wave through pedestrians.
9. Demonstrator: **DIRECTION**—show verified site-approved directional cue, replay from side.
10. Pedestrian conflict: stop movement, protect crossing and verify route clear.
11. Loss of visibility/communication: stop operation; do not improvise signals.
12. Reverse movement: use authorized banksman/spotter procedure; no blind-spot entry.
13. Two-signaler conflict: stop movement and re-establish one clear controller.
14. Interactive pause: learner chooses safe signal/decision for approaching vehicle.
15. Review: match verified signal, driver response, safety check and escalation.

## Interaction & evaluation
- Show a short motion clip, then freeze with the question: 'What should the signaler do now? / ماذا يجب أن يفعل منظم الحركة الآن؟'
- Learner chooses between approved site-signal alternatives and safe stop/escalation; show feedback explaining hazards and why alternatives are unsafe.
- Five randomized bilingual knowledge-check questions; minimum 4/5 (80%) to advance.
- No certificate or trainee data in pilot. Keep all production courses/certificates untouched.
