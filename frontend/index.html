About Me

Abdallah Abdelaziz Shalaby is a certified Security Trainer and LMS Specialist with 17+ years of professional experience across the GCC, including Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar.

He has trained more than 3,000 security professionals and has practical experience in security operations, fire safety, emergency response, incident management, security inspections, crowd management and workforce development. His current work combines training operations, LMS administration, digital learning, scheduling, reporting and quality support.

His professional trainer credentials and affiliations include Highfield, ASSD, PSFRD and IFPO, in addition to membership in PSSA. He is committed to structured, practical and high-impact learning that translates professional standards into confident performance in real working environments.
