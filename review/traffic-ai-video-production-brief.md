## Global prompt
Create a realistic instructional video at a private facility checkpoint. A qualified security traffic marshal in a navy uniform and reflective vest stands outside the vehicle's swept path. Use full-body static camera, front view and driver POV, with both hands visible. Realistic fingers, wrists, elbows, palms, continuity and vehicle behavior. No police uniforms, police gestures, invented signs, narration, music, subtitles or synthetic logos. **Animate the specific hand gesture only using the supplied handbook reference figure**. If exact movement cannot be reproduced, reject output. Film vehicle maneuvers as controlled simulations. 16:9, 1080p if available. Make 3 clips per maneuver: setup (5–8s), gesture (8–12s), driver response (5–8s). Assemble and narrate separately.
